# 👋 About Me

I am **Zizhen Li (李子桢)**, a Ph.D. student at the [College of Artificial Intelligence, Nankai University](https://ai.nankai.edu.cn/), jointly trained at [Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII)](https://www.sii.edu.cn/), advised by [Kaipeng Zhang](https://kpzhang93.github.io/). I began my graduate study at Nankai University in 2022 and transferred to the Ph.D. program in 2024. I received my B.Eng. from Taiyuan University of Technology in 2022.

My research interests lie at **Human–AI Co-Creation for Game Design** and **LLMs for Social and Entertainment Scenarios**.

<p class="games-line">🎲 Off the clock, I play social deduction (Avalon, One Night Werewolf, Blood on the Clocktower), card and tile games (Sheng Ji, Texas Hold'em, Mahjong), and light strategy games (Catan, Codenames, Cabo).</p>
